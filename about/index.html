About RideFlow

RideFlow is a transportation dispatch and management web application that helps transportation companies organize ride requests, assign drivers, and run day-to-day operations.

What RideFlow does

Transportation companies use RideFlow to replace phone calls, spreadsheets, and scattered messages with a single system for scheduled rides. Each company has its own RideFlow account; its staff sign in with their own logins, and company data stays within that company's account.

  • Dispatchers create and manage ride requests, including passenger details, pickup and drop-off locations, scheduled date and time, and vehicle type.
  • Dispatchers select an available driver for a ride. RideFlow checks the driver's existing schedule to avoid double-booking.
  • Drivers who have opted in receive a text message about the ride offer and confirm or decline it from a secure one-time link.
  • Managers manage drivers, vehicles, and pricing rules, and review operational reports.
  • RideFlow calculates ride pricing from route distance, vehicle type, the company's pricing rules, and hourly duration where applicable.

Who uses RideFlow

RideFlow is business software. It is used by transportation companies and the people who work with them, not by the general public.

Transportation companies

A company registers for RideFlow and becomes the account owner for its own drivers, vehicles, pricing rules, rides, and reports.

Managers

Managers add drivers to the company's roster, record whether each driver has agreed to receive SMS notifications, and manage vehicles, pricing, and reporting.

Dispatchers

Dispatchers handle incoming ride requests, keep ride details up to date, and offer rides to available drivers.

Drivers

Drivers do not need a RideFlow login. They receive ride offers by text message (if they have opted in) and respond through a secure link that works for that one ride only.

Our approach to messaging and privacy

RideFlow only texts drivers about rides that are offered to them, and only after the driver's consent has been recorded. We never use phone numbers for marketing.
