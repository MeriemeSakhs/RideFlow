Messaging

SMS Information & Consent

RideFlow sends transactional text messages to drivers about rides offered to them. This page explains who receives these messages, why, how consent is given and recorded, and how to stop them.

Message type

Transactional ride assignment notifications only. No marketing or promotional messages.

Recipients

Drivers who work with a transportation company that uses RideFlow and who have agreed to receive ride notifications.

Frequency

Message frequency varies based on ride activity and the notifications associated with your RideFlow account.

Why RideFlow sends text messages

Drivers do not sign in to RideFlow. Text messages are how they learn that a ride has been offered to them. The process is:

  1. A dispatcher creates a ride request in RideFlow.
  2. The dispatcher selects an available driver for the ride.
  3. RideFlow records the ride offer for that driver.
  4. If the driver has opted in to SMS, RideFlow texts the driver's phone number with the passenger name, pickup and drop-off locations, the scheduled date and time, and a secure one-time link.
  5. The driver uses the link to review the ride and confirm or decline it.
  6. RideFlow may also send other transactional notifications about the status of a ride offered to or assigned to the driver.

If a driver has not opted in, RideFlow does not text them. The dispatcher is told that no message was sent and contacts the driver directly.

Sample message

RideFlow: New ride request. Passenger: Jane D. Pickup: 125 Main St on Oct 3, 2026 at 9:30 AM. Drop-off: 400 Airport Rd. Confirm or decline: [secure one-time RideFlow link] Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Example of a ride assignment text. Names, addresses, and times are illustrative.

How drivers opt in

RideFlow is used by transportation companies, and each company's manager adds its drivers to RideFlow. A driver's consent is collected and recorded as follows:

  1. The driver agrees. Before any text is sent, the driver tells their transportation company that they agree to receive RideFlow ride assignment text messages at their mobile number, for example in writing during onboarding.
  2. The manager records it. When adding the driver in the RideFlow Manager Portal, the manager enters the driver's mobile number and checks the separate SMS consent checkbox shown here. The checkbox is not pre-selected, is optional, and a driver can be added without checking it.
  3. Consent can be recorded or withdrawn later. From the driver's details, a manager can record a driver's consent after the driver agrees, or withdraw it at the driver's request. RideFlow stores the date consent was recorded.

SMS consent is separate from any acceptance of the Terms & Conditions or acknowledgement of the Privacy Policy, and it is not required to work with a transportation company that uses RideFlow.

Help and opting out

Get help

Reply HELP for help or contact RideFlow support through the Contact page.

Stop messages

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications.

After you reply STOP, RideFlow will not send you further text messages unless you opt in again. You can also ask your transportation company's manager to withdraw your SMS consent in RideFlow.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on ride activity and the notifications associated with your RideFlow account. Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
