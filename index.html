Operations in motion

Dispatch rides and keep drivers informed, all in one place.

RideFlow is a transportation dispatch and management web application for transportation companies. Dispatchers create ride requests and assign drivers, drivers confirm rides from a secure link, and managers oversee vehicles, pricing, and reports.

Built for the people who keep rides moving

RideFlow is used by transportation companies and their staff. Each company works in its own account, and each role sees only the tools it needs.

Dispatchers

Create and manage ride requests with passenger details, pickup and drop-off locations, and scheduled times, then offer each ride to an available driver.

Drivers

Receive a text message when a ride is offered to them, review the ride details, and confirm or decline it from a secure one-time link. No app download is required.

Managers

Manage the driver roster, vehicles, and pricing rules, and review operational reports on ride activity and completed rides.

Driver notifications

Ride assignment texts, not marketing

When a dispatcher offers a ride to a driver who has opted in to SMS notifications, RideFlow sends that driver one text message with the ride's pickup and drop-off details and a link to confirm or decline it.

RideFlow's text messages are transactional notifications about ride assignments. RideFlow does not send promotional or marketing text messages.

Pricing calculated from the ride itself

RideFlow estimates each ride's price from the route distance between pickup and drop-off, the vehicle type, the company's pricing rules, and, for hourly bookings, the ride's duration.
