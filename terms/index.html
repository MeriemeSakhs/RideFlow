Description of the service

RideFlow helps transportation companies ("Companies") manage scheduled rides. The Service lets:

  • dispatchers create and manage ride requests and select an available driver for each ride;
  • drivers receive ride assignment notifications and confirm or decline rides offered to them;
  • managers manage drivers, vehicles, pricing rules, and operational reports; and

Ride requests and driver assignment

Dispatchers are responsible for entering accurate ride details, including passenger information, pickup and drop-off locations, and scheduled times. When a dispatcher selects a driver, RideFlow checks the driver's existing scheduled rides to help avoid conflicts and offers the ride to that driver. The ride is pending until the driver confirms it. A driver may decline a ride, and confirmation links expire and work only once.

Address suggestions, map locations, and route distances come from third-party mapping services and may occasionally be inaccurate. Companies should verify ride details before dispatching.

Pricing information

RideFlow calculates ride prices from the pricing rules each Company configures, the vehicle type, route distance, and, for hourly rides, duration. These prices are estimates produced by the Company's own rules. Each Company is solely responsible for its pricing rules, for the prices it quotes or charges its passengers, and for any taxes or fees. RideFlow does not process passenger payments.
