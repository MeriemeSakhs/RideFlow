Program description

RideFlow SMS messages are transactional, service-related notifications associated with ride management and driver assignments. When a dispatcher at a transportation company offers a ride to a driver, RideFlow texts that driver the ride details and a secure link to confirm or decline the ride. RideFlow may also send other transactional notifications related to the status of a ride offered to or assigned to the driver.

RideFlow does not send promotional or marketing messages.

Example message:

RideFlow: New ride request. Passenger: Jane D. Pickup: 125 Main St on Oct 3, 2026 at 9:30 AM. Drop-off: 400 Airport Rd. Confirm or decline: [secure one-time RideFlow link] Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Who receives messages

Message recipients are drivers, or other authorized users, whose phone numbers are used for RideFlow operational notifications by a transportation company that uses RideFlow, and who have agreed to receive these messages. Consent is recorded in RideFlow before any message is sent; drivers without recorded consent are not texted. See how drivers opt in.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on ride activity and the notifications associated with your RideFlow account.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Charges depend on your mobile plan; contact your wireless carrier for details.

STOP

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications.

After you opt out, you will not receive further RideFlow text messages unless you opt in again. To opt back in, reply START to the RideFlow number, and ask your transportation company's manager to record your consent in RideFlow.

Opting out of SMS does not delete your RideFlow account or your transportation company's account, and does not remove you as a driver. It does mean you will no longer receive ride-related SMS notifications, such as new ride offers and confirmation links, so your company will need to contact you another way about rides.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
