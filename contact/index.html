Support

Contact & Support

Questions about RideFlow, ride notifications, or your information? We're here to help.

Email support

Email us at rideflow.notifications@gmail.com. Please include your name, your transportation company, and, for SMS questions, the phone number that receives RideFlow messages.

Help by text message

Reply HELP to any RideFlow text for help.

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS notifications.

Common requests

Stop ride texts

Reply STOP to a RideFlow message, or email us. Your manager can also withdraw your SMS consent in RideFlow. See the SMS Terms.

Account and login

Managers and dispatchers can update their profile in RideFlow. For login problems, email us from the address on your account.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, or delete your information, email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Drivers: questions about a specific ride, such as pickup times or changes, are best answered by the dispatcher at your transportation company.
