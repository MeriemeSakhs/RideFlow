Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RideFlow ("RideFlow," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when transportation companies, their dispatchers and managers, and their drivers use the RideFlow platform to manage ride dispatch and fleet operations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: full name, email address, date of birth, company name, and a securely hashed password, provided when a transportation company signs up for RideFlow.
  • Driver information: a driver's name, mobile phone number, and driver's license number, entered by a fleet manager when adding the driver to the platform, along with the driver's SMS consent status and the date consent was given.
  • Ride and passenger information: passenger name and phone number, pickup and drop-off addresses (and corresponding map coordinates when available), vehicle type, ride date and time, and ride status, entered by a dispatcher when creating a ride.
  • Vehicle and pricing information: vehicle types and pricing configured by a manager for their company's fleet.
  • Usage information: standard technical data such as IP address and browser type, collected automatically when the platform is used.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the ride dispatch platform: creating, assigning, tracking, and completing rides.
  • To notify drivers of new ride assignments and let them confirm or decline, including by text message where consent has been given (see Section 3).
  • To verify accounts and secure access (for example, email verification codes at signup).
  • To generate operational reporting and dashboards for a company's own managers and dispatchers.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform.

All ride, driver, vehicle, and pricing data belongs to, and is only ever visible to, the transportation company that created it. RideFlow is multi-tenant: one company's data is never shown to another company.

3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

With a driver's consent, RideFlow sends ride-assignment text messages to that driver's mobile phone number. These messages notify the driver of a new ride request and include a link to confirm or decline the assignment.

Consent and frequency. A driver's enrollment in this messaging program is optional and is not required to be added to a company's roster. Message frequency varies based on ride activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, or STOP at any time to opt out of further messages.
We do not sell, share, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in / messaging consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile information is used solely to operate RideFlow's ride-assignment messaging program described above, and is shared only with the messaging service provider (Twilio) strictly to deliver these messages on RideFlow's behalf.

Consent to receive these messages is collected directly for this specific program — see our SMS consent script for exactly how and when consent is obtained — and is never inferred from, or shared with, any other messaging program.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as necessary to operate the platform:

  • With infrastructure providers that host our application and database (e.g. MongoDB Atlas).
  • With Twilio, solely to deliver the SMS messages described in Section 3.
  • With our email delivery provider, solely to send account verification emails.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of RideFlow, our customers, or others.

5. Data Retention

We retain account, driver, ride, and vehicle data for as long as a company's RideFlow account is active and as needed to provide the service. A driver's SMS consent status is retained until the driver (or their managing company) withdraws it, at which point no further messages are sent to that driver.

6. Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never stored in plain text. Access to a company's data is restricted to authenticated users of that company. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access.

7. Your Choices

  • A driver may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP, or by asking their managing company to update their consent status in RideFlow.
  • Account holders may contact us (Section 9) to request access to, or deletion of, their information, subject to applicable law and our operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

RideFlow is a business operations tool intended for use by transportation companies and their staff. It is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect information from, individuals under 18.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to rideflow.notifications@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
