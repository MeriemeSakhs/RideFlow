Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how RideFlow ("RideFlow," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information in connection with the RideFlow transportation dispatch and management web application, the text messages it sends, and this website (together, the "Service").

RideFlow is business software used by transportation companies ("Companies") and their managers, dispatchers, and drivers. This policy applies to everyone whose information is processed through the Service, including Company staff with RideFlow logins, drivers who receive ride notifications, and passengers whose ride details are entered by a Company.

Information we collect

Account information

When a Company registers or a manager or dispatcher account is created, we collect the person's full name, email address, phone number (if provided), role (manager or dispatcher), the Company they belong to, and a password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in readable form. We also send a verification code to confirm email addresses.

Driver information

When a manager adds a driver, we collect the driver's name, mobile phone number, driver's license number, availability status, whether the driver has agreed to receive SMS notifications, and the date that consent was recorded.

Ride information

When a dispatcher or manager creates a ride, we collect the passenger's name and phone number, pickup and drop-off addresses, the map coordinates of those addresses, the scheduled date and time, estimated duration, vehicle type, notes entered by the dispatcher, the assigned driver, ride status (for example requested, pending, confirmed, completed, or cancelled), and the calculated price.

Vehicle and pricing information

Managers enter vehicle details and pricing rules for their Company. This is business information, not personal information, but it is stored with the Company's account.

Phone numbers and SMS records

We use driver mobile numbers to send ride assignment text messages to drivers who have opted in. Our SMS provider processes the phone number, message content, and delivery status of each message, as well as opt-out replies such as STOP.

Device and technical information

When you use the RideFlow web application, your browser stores a sign-in token so you stay logged in. Our servers and service providers may process standard technical information needed to deliver the Service, such as IP address, browser type, and request times. RideFlow does not use advertising cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics on this website or in the application.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: creating and managing accounts, ride requests, drivers, vehicles, pricing rules, and reports for each Company.
  • To manage rides: ride information is used to schedule rides, check driver availability and avoid double-booking, show rides on a map, calculate route distance and price, let drivers confirm or decline rides, and produce operational reports for the Company's managers.
  • To send transactional SMS notifications: see the next section.
  • To send account emails: such as email verification codes. We do not send marketing email.
  • To secure the Service: authenticating users, enforcing role-based access, and preventing misuse.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

How we use phone numbers for SMS

RideFlow sends text messages only for transactional, service-related purposes. When a dispatcher offers a ride to a driver who has opted in, RideFlow texts that driver the passenger name, pickup and drop-off locations, scheduled date and time, and a secure one-time link to confirm or decline the ride. RideFlow may also send other transactional notifications about the status of a ride offered to or assigned to that driver.

RideFlow does not send marketing or promotional text messages. Drivers who have not opted in are not texted. Passenger phone numbers are stored for the Company's dispatch use and are not texted by RideFlow.

Message frequency varies based on ride activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to opt out. See our SMS Terms and SMS Information page for how consent is collected.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent are not shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information is shared with our SMS provider only to deliver the messages described above.

How we protect information

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • Access is restricted by role (manager or dispatcher) and limited to the user's own Company.
  • Driver confirmation links are random, single-use, tied to one ride, expire automatically, and are stored only in hashed form.
  • Service credentials, such as database and SMS provider keys, are kept on the server and are never exposed to browsers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password confidential and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

Data retention

We keep account, driver, and ride information for as long as the Company's RideFlow account is active and as needed to provide ride history and reports, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When a manager deletes a driver, the driver is deactivated rather than erased so past rides remain accurate. Driver confirmation links and email verification codes expire automatically. When a Company closes its account, or you ask us to delete your information, we delete or de-identify it unless we must keep it for legal reasons. SMS delivery records held by our SMS provider are retained according to that provider's policies.

Your rights and choices

  • Stop text messages: reply STOP to any RideFlow text, or ask your Company's manager to withdraw your SMS consent in RideFlow. Opting out of SMS does not delete your information or your Company's RideFlow account.
  • Access and correction: managers and dispatchers can view and update their profile information in RideFlow. Drivers and passengers can ask the Company that entered their information, or contact us, to review or correct it.
  • Deletion: you may ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal or legitimate record-keeping needs.
  • Additional rights: depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. We will respond to requests as those laws require.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. Because Companies control the information they enter into RideFlow, we may refer requests to, or work with, the relevant Company.

Children's privacy

RideFlow is business software intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children for account or SMS purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service changes. We will post the updated policy at this same address and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we use phone numbers or SMS consent, we will provide notice before the change takes effect.
